Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

How Pick10 collects, uses, and protects your information / 了解 Pick10 如何收集、使用与保护您的信息

Last updated / 最后更新：June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pick10 ("Make 10", "we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our iOS mobile application (Bundle ID: com.lixiao.game.make10).

本隐私政策说明 Pick10（Make 10，「我们」或「本应用」）在您使用 iOS 移动应用（Bundle ID：com.lixiao.game.make10）时如何收集、使用与共享信息。

1. Information We Collect / 我们收集的信息

Pick10 does not require account registration. We may collect the following through the App and third-party services:

  • Device information — device model, OS version, language, and general device settings
    设备信息 — 设备型号、系统版本、语言及基本设备设置
  • Advertising identifiers — IDFA on iOS when you grant App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission
    广告标识符 — 在您授权 ATT 后，iOS 上的 IDFA；用于个性化广告与广告效果衡量
  • Usage data — app interactions, session data, and performance metrics
    使用数据 — 应用交互、会话数据与性能指标，用于改进稳定性
  • Game Center data — scores, achievements, and leaderboard rankings
    Game Center 数据 — 使用 Apple Game Center 功能时的分数、成就与排行榜排名

2. Device Permissions / 设备权限

  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT) — for Google AdMob ads
    应用跟踪透明度 — 通过 Google AdMob 展示更相关广告并衡量广告效果
  • Apple Game Center — sync scores and achievements
    Apple Game Center — 与 Apple ID 同步分数、成就与排行榜

We do NOT use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or Bluetooth.

我们不使用摄像头、麦克风、位置、通讯录、照片或蓝牙。

3. Third-Party Services / 第三方服务

  • Google AdMob — interstitial advertisements
    展示插屏广告
  • Google UMP — privacy/consent dialogs
    广告同意弹窗
  • Apple Game Center — leaderboards and achievements
    排行榜与成就
  • SKAdNetwork — ad attribution via AdMob
    Apple 隐私保护广告归因框架
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & Advertising
  • Apple Privacy Policy

4. How We Use Information / 信息用途

  • Display and measure advertisements (Google AdMob) / 展示与衡量广告效果
  • Present ad consent dialogs (Google UMP) / 展示广告同意弹窗
  • Sync game scores and achievements (Game Center) / 同步游戏分数与成就
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs / 改进性能与修复问题

5. Data Sharing / 数据共享

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with Google (AdMob, UMP), Apple (Game Center), and service providers only as necessary.

我们不会出售您的个人信息。数据可能与 Google、Apple 及协助运营的服务提供商共享，且仅限于提供服务所必需的范围。

6. Your Choices / 您的选择

  • ATT: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
    设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪
  • Limit Ad Tracking: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising
    设置 → 隐私与安全性 → Apple 广告
  • Game Center: Settings → Game Center
    设置 → Game Center

7. Children's Privacy / 儿童隐私

Pick10 is not directed at children under 13.

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。
