Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Last updated / 最后更新：June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pick10 ("Make 10", "we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our iOS mobile application (Bundle ID: com.lixiao.game.make10).

本隐私政策说明 Pick10（Make 10，「我们」或「本应用」）在您使用 iOS 移动应用（Bundle ID：com.lixiao.game.make10）时如何收集、使用与共享信息。

1. Information We Collect / 我们收集的信息

Pick10 does not require account registration. We may collect the following through the App and third-party services:

  • Device information — device model, OS version, language, and general device settings
    设备信息 — 设备型号、系统版本、语言及基本设备设置
  • Advertising identifiers — IDFA on iOS when you grant App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission; used for personalized ads and ad measurement
    广告标识符 — 在您授权 App 跟踪透明度（ATT）后，iOS 上的 IDFA；用于个性化广告与广告效果衡量
  • Usage data — app interactions, session data, and performance metrics to improve stability
    使用数据 — 应用交互、会话数据与性能指标，用于改进稳定性
  • Game Center data — scores, achievements, and leaderboard rankings when you use Apple Game Center features
    Game Center 数据 — 使用 Apple Game Center 功能时的分数、成就与排行榜排名

Pick10 无需注册账号。我们可能通过应用及第三方服务收集上述信息。

2. Device Permissions / 设备权限

The App may request the following permissions on iOS:

  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT) — to deliver more relevant ads and measure ad performance via Google AdMob
    应用跟踪透明度 — 通过 Google AdMob 展示更相关广告并衡量广告效果
  • Apple Game Center — to sync scores, achievements, and leaderboards with your Apple ID
    Apple Game Center — 与 Apple ID 同步分数、成就与排行榜

We do NOT use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or Bluetooth. If these keys appear in the system manifest for build compliance, the App does not access those features.

我们不使用摄像头、麦克风、位置、通讯录、照片或蓝牙。若系统清单中出现相关键值仅为构建合规，本应用不会访问这些功能。

3. Third-Party Services / 第三方服务

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — displays interstitial advertisements; may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad delivery and measurement
    展示插屏广告；可能收集设备标识符与使用数据用于广告投放与衡量
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — presents privacy/consent choices required in certain regions before ads are shown
    在特定地区展示广告前，提供隐私/同意选择界面
  • Apple Game Center — leaderboards and achievements; governed by Apple's privacy policy
    排行榜与成就；受 Apple 隐私政策约束
  • SKAdNetwork — Apple's privacy-preserving ad attribution framework used by AdMob
    Apple 隐私保护广告归因框架，由 AdMob 使用

Related policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & Advertising
  • Apple Privacy Policy

4. How We Use Information / 信息用途

  • Display and measure advertisements (Google AdMob) / 展示与衡量广告效果
  • Present ad consent dialogs (Google UMP) / 展示广告同意弹窗
  • Sync game scores and achievements (Game Center) / 同步游戏分数与成就
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs / 改进性能与修复问题
  • Comply with legal obligations / 履行法律义务

5. Data Sharing / 数据共享

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with Google (AdMob, UMP), Apple (Game Center), and service providers who assist in operating the App, only as necessary to provide the service.

我们不会出售您的个人信息。数据可能与 Google（AdMob、UMP）、Apple（Game Center）及协助运营的服务提供商共享，且仅限于提供服务所必需的范围。

6. Your Choices / 您的选择

  • ATT / 跟踪权限: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking — allow or deny app tracking
    设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪 — 允许或拒绝应用跟踪
  • Limit Ad Tracking / 限制广告跟踪: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising
    设置 → 隐私与安全性 → Apple 广告
  • Game Center: Settings → Game Center — sign out or manage your profile
    设置 → Game Center — 退出登录或管理个人资料

7. Data Retention / 数据保留

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide the App and comply with legal obligations. Third-party services may retain data according to their own policies.

我们仅在提供服务及履行法律义务所需期间保留信息。第三方服务依照其自身政策保留数据。

8. Children's Privacy / 儿童隐私

Pick10 is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集该年龄段儿童的个人信息。
