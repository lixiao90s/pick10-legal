FAQ / 常见问题

  • How do I start? Open the app and tap Play to begin a new game.
    如何开始游戏？ 打开应用，点击开始即可进入新一局。
  • Why do I see ads? Pick10 is free and supported by Google AdMob interstitial ads. You can continue playing after ads finish.
    为什么有广告？ 本应用免费运营，通过 Google AdMob 插屏广告维持服务，广告结束后可继续游戏。
  • Why am I asked about tracking? On iOS, the App may request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission to show more relevant ads. You can decline in Settings → Privacy → Tracking.
    为什么询问跟踪权限？ iOS 上可能请求 ATT 权限以展示更相关广告，您可在「设置 → 隐私 → 跟踪」中拒绝。
  • How do Game Center leaderboards work? Sign in to Game Center on your device. Your best scores are reported to the global leaderboard automatically.
    Game Center 排行榜如何工作？ 在设备上登录 Game Center，最高分将自动上报至全球排行榜。
  • Does the app use my camera or location? No. Pick10 does not access camera, microphone, location, or contacts.
    应用会使用摄像头或位置吗？ 不会。Pick10 不访问摄像头、麦克风、位置或通讯录。
  • How do I report a problem? Email 393889869@qq.com with your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue.
    如何反馈问题？ 请发送邮件并注明设备型号、iOS 版本及问题描述。
